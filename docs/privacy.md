# What leaves your machine

To choose, Relay sends the decider's provider your message text (at most 4,000 characters), the end of the chat's last reply when it has to choose between your models, by your "use it for" lines or by how big the work is (at most 1,500 characters of the text the model wrote, with your home folder written as `~`; never its reasoning, tool calls or tool output), when it asks how big the work is, the start of the chat's first message, which states the task (at most 1,500 characters, with your home folder written as `~`; not when it is the message itself), the names and sizes of attached files (not their contents), how many pictures are attached, whether the chat's agent can change files, and your "use it for" lines. It never sends file contents, pictures, other earlier messages (only the first, as above), model names, prices or index scores: when Relay chooses between models by itself, it asks only how big the work is, and ranks the models on your machine. When a model starts a helper, the helper's task text is sent the same way; that text is written by the model and may quote code or chat content you do not see.

For the Relay usage tab, Relay reads the same numbers for every reply of every chat when the chat finishes, and just before OpenCode summarizes a long chat: the token counts, the cost, the time of the message, the model and agent that answered, whether Relay placed the message with its short note on that choice (for example "small work, so the cheapest", or an error it met), and the project's folder. It keeps them in OpenCode's plugin storage on your machine for 90 days, sends them nowhere, and never keeps the text of a message or reply, a file name or a chat's title.

To weigh a move and to show what a reply took, Relay reads the chat's token counts, costs and times, the model that answered, and that model's listed prices from OpenCode on your machine. It keeps them with its last 20 records in OpenCode's plugin storage and sends them nowhere. It never stores the text of your messages.

To show the intelligence index, and a price and context size where OpenCode lists none, Relay downloads OpenRouter's public model list, at most once a day. It sends no key and nothing about you or your models.

If you save an Artificial Analysis key in the panel, Relay also downloads Artificial Analysis's free model list with it, at most once a day, for prices and the index; OpenRouter's list is still downloaded, without the key, for the models that list has no index for. It sends the key and nothing else. The key is kept in OpenCode's plugin storage on your machine, sent only to Artificial Analysis, and never shown in the panel again. Remove deletes it. [Data sources](data-sources.md) has the details.

OpenCode makes every model request, with your OpenCode login. Relay never sees your API keys.
