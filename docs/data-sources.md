# Data sources

Relay shows each model's price as OpenCode lists it in its own model catalog (the host's `model.list`; OpenCode fills it from models.dev). For a model OpenCode lists no price for, Relay shows another price, in this order: $0 for a model OpenCode offers free by its name (an id ending `-free` or `:free`, or Free in its name), then Artificial Analysis's (with a key, below), then OpenRouter's (below). Each is the model's price on its maker's API and is marked `*`, with its source in the tooltip; with a Claude login your plan pays instead. The context size is OpenCode's, else OpenRouter's. A provider's own `cost` in an `opencode.json` file is part of that catalog too. The panel shows the base price per 1M tokens, input / output, in its model tables and beside each chosen model.

- A price of 0 in and 0 out reads "$0 or not listed": the catalog gives 0 both for a free model and for a model with no listed price, so Relay never calls a model free.
- A model with no price at all reads "price not listed". On 17 Nov 2026 that was every model of OpenCode's `claude-agent` provider.
- The tables' "Cheapest first" order compares the input price, then the output price, as exact decimals; a price not listed sorts last.

## Intelligence index

Since 18 Nov 2026 the model tables show the **Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index** of each model Relay can match, taken from OpenRouter's public model list (`https://openrouter.ai/api/v1/models`, the `benchmarks.artificial_analysis.intelligence_index` field). Since 26 Nov 2026 Relay also keeps every model's price (`pricing`, USD a token, kept as USD for a million tokens) and context (`context_length`) from that list, for models OpenCode lists none for.

- **What leaves the machine.** One plain GET of that public list, with no key, no cookie and nothing about the person or their models. It is fetched when the panel asks for the index and at most once a day: the result is kept in Relay's plugin storage, and a failed fetch falls back to the stored copy. A reply with no index at all is treated as a failure, so it cannot replace a good copy.
- **How models are matched.** OpenCode's model records share no id with OpenRouter's or Artificial Analysis's list, so one ladder (`findModel` in `src/intelligence.ts`) finds a model in either, by its id, then its name. Both sides are put in one form first: no vendor, lower case, dashes for dots, colons and spaces, and no variant suffix such as ":batch", ":free" or OpenCode's "-free". Then, step by step: as written; without a date at the end (`claude-haiku-4-5-20251001` is `claude-haiku-4.5`); with the words in any order and the numbers in theirs (`claude-4-5-haiku`, but `gpt-5-4-mini` is never `gpt-4-5-mini`); and the one listed model whose id ends in it (`Sonnet 5.5` is `claude-sonnet-5.5`). A step that two different listed models fit matches nothing, and the table shows "—". A match without what is wanted, such as an entry with no index, gives way to the next name or step. Nothing in it names a provider, so a new provider's models are found the same way. The cell's tooltip names the listed model each number came from.
- **What the number means.** Artificial Analysis tests each effort of a model on its own, and the scores differ a lot. On 18 Nov 2026 its pages gave GPT-6.2 Sol 57 at max, 56 at xhigh, 55 at high, 53 at medium and 41 at low. OpenRouter lists one score per model and does not say for which effort. Its 57.3 for GPT-6.2 Sol, 54.5 for Claude Sonnet 5.5 (55 at max, 26 at low) and 51.3 for GPT-6.1 Terra (51 at max, 29 at low) each matched the highest effort, to the nearest point, since Artificial Analysis's pages show whole numbers. So for a model with efforts, the panel says the number is for its highest effort. OpenRouter states no index version.
- **Effort and price.** The price per token is the same at every effort: GPT-6.2 Sol costs $2 in and $10 out per 1M tokens at each one. OpenRouter's higher prices for it are faster service tiers and regional endpoints. A higher effort writes more tokens, so each reply costs more. On Artificial Analysis's test, GPT-6.2 Sol wrote 60.9M output tokens at max and 7.21M at low.
- **Per-effort numbers.** Only Artificial Analysis's own API lists each effort, with its index and what running the index cost. Its documentation on 18 Nov 2026 said it needs a free key, allows 100 requests a day, and asks for a visible credit to Artificial Analysis. Since 19 Nov 2026 Relay uses it when the owner saves a key (below).
- **Coverage.** Measured on 18 Nov 2026 against models.dev's lists, it matched 15 of 27 Ollama Cloud models and 8 of 18 Anthropic models. OpenCode's Claude Agent models Sonnet 5.5, Haiku 5.5 and Opus 5.5 matched by name. Joining on models.dev's `canonical_model_id` would add a few more (17 of 27 Ollama Cloud models), but it means a 6.7 MB download, so it is not done.
- **Terms.** Artificial Analysis limits reuse of its data. Relay copies none of it into this repository or the package: each person's own Relay reads OpenRouter's public list at run time, as OpenRouter's own site shows it. The owner asked for this on 18 Nov 2026. This is a reading, not legal advice.

## Prices and index from Artificial Analysis, with your key

Since 19 Nov 2026, with an Artificial Analysis key saved in the panel, Relay downloads Artificial Analysis's free model list (`https://artificialanalysis.ai/api/v2/language/models/free`, every page, with the key in `x-api-key`).

- **When.** It downloads when the model tables load the index and when a key is saved, at most once a day; the copy is kept in plugin storage. A refused key or a used-up day keeps the older copy and says why.
- **What it gives.** For each model and effort: the price for a million tokens in, out, read from a cache and written to a cache, and the intelligence index. Checked on 19 Nov 2026 with the owner's test key: 763 models, an index for 156, input and output prices for 475, a cache-read price for 364 and a cache-write price for 131.
- **Prices.** A reply is priced with Artificial Analysis's prices only when OpenCode lists none for its model. They are marked `*` in the Relay usage tab.
- **Matching.** Each effort is listed on its own: `claude-sonnet-5-5` is Max and `claude-sonnet-5-5-high` is High. Relay matches the model's id or name exactly, with its effort first. The subscription login's `sonnet`, `haiku`, `opus` and `fable` stand for the newest Anthropic model of that name.
- **The index.** With a key, the model tables show Artificial Analysis's index, and OpenRouter's copy of it for a model that list has no index for. Without one, or when its list cannot be had, OpenRouter's.
- **Each effort.** Artificial Analysis lists each effort as its own entry: its plain entry is the highest effort (OpenCode's `max`), and OpenCode's `none` is its non-reasoning entry. The line under a chosen model gives its effort's own index when there is one, and otherwise the highest effort's, saying so.
- **Credit.** Artificial Analysis asks for a visible credit wherever its data is shown. Relay shows "Prices and index: Artificial Analysis (artificialanalysis.ai)" in the Relay usage tab and in `/relay-stats`, and names it as the index's source.

The older pipeline's optional OpenRouter price catalog and its separate Artificial Analysis panel, which needed a key, were removed on 17 Nov 2026.
